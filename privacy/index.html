Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

This policy explains how Digital House Group ("we", "us") handles personal information when you use our THREADHAUS online booking service at digitalhousegroup.co.za and related pages (the "Service"). We process personal information in line with South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

1. Who is responsible

The Service lets you book appointments, services and orders with a business. We operate the Service. The business you book with receives your booking details and uses them to serve you.

2. What we collect

  • Booking details: your full name, phone number, optional email address, optional notes, and the service, date and time you choose.
  • Account details: your email address and password if you create an account.
  • Booking status and reference: the reference code we generate and the status of your booking (pending, confirmed, completed, cancelled).
  • On your device: your booking reference codes are stored in your browser's local storage so that "My Bookings" can show your bookings on that device.

We do not ask for payment card details in the booking form.

3. How we use your information

  • To create, confirm, reschedule, track and cancel your booking.
  • To let the business contact you about your booking.
  • To operate, secure and improve the Service.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Who we share it with

  • The business you book with, which sees your booking details in its dashboard.
  • Service providers that host and run the Service on our behalf.
  • Google Fonts: our pages load fonts from Google, which means your browser connects to Google's servers and Google receives your IP address.
  • Authorities, where the law requires it.

5. Google user data

If you choose to sign in with Google, we receive only your basic profile information (your name, email address and profile picture, where provided) so we can identify your account. We use it only to provide and secure the Service. We do not sell it, use it for advertising, or share it except as needed to run the Service or as the law requires. Our use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. How long we keep it

We keep booking and account information for as long as it is needed to provide the Service and meet legal or accounting requirements. You can ask us to delete it sooner (see section 8).

7. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational steps to protect your information. No online service is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your rights

Under POPIA you may ask us to confirm what personal information we hold about you, and to correct or delete it. You may also object to processing. To do so, email us at the address below. If you are unhappy with how we handle your request, you can complain to the Information Regulator (South Africa) at inforegulator.org.za.

9. Children

The Service is not intended for children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date above shows when it last changed.
